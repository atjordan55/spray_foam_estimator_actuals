Let's clarify before proceeding.

We have material in or inventory that falls into various categories but essentially:
- It is material we paid for that hasn't yet been paid for by a job (Not Surplus Material)
- It is material that is in excess from a job that has already paid for it (Surplus Material)

For example we can have a total of 300 gallons of material in inventory
- 200 gallons may fall into the (Not Surplus Material) category
- 100 gallons may fall into the (Surplus Material) category

We have 300 gallons of material but would treat 200 gallons of it differently than we would treat the remaining 100 gallons. 

- Any gallons coming from the Surplus Material requires a commitment via 'Apply Inventory Credits'
- When calculating gallons of material needed for a job vs what is in the inventory - always make the distinction by letting the user know how much would need to come from (Surplus Material 'Apply Inventory Credits') if there isn't enough (Not Surplus Material) to cover the job

- Primary reason for using Surplus Material is to provide more competitive pricing for specific customers in cases where we deem it necessary to win the job

- Secondary reason for using Surplus Material would be just to utilize the existing material inventory we have without having to order more prior to completing a job
